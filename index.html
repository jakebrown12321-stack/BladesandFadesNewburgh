About

I’m Jess, owner of Blades and Fades. Been grinding since 2020, putting in the work, perfecting the craft, and earning the trust of every client who sits in my chair. This isn’t rushed, and it’s not basic. Every cut is dialed in, clean, and styled to whatever you can throw at me. No cutting corners. No uneven fades. Just real attention to detail and a standard I don’t break. Whether you’re coming in for a fresh fade, a clean-up, or a whole new look! I make sure you walk out sharper than when you came in. Come as you are. Leave feeling your best.
